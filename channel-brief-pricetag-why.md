# Pricetag Why — channel brief

The owner's brief for the channel that replaces Dark Outliers (pasted 2026-10-31). Code: `modules/pw_*.py`,
dashboard tab "Pricetag Why", scripts in `content/pricetagwhy/scripts/`.

**Name:** Pricetag Why (@PricetagWhy), working name; backup "Money Why". Check the handle is free.

**Concept:** whiteboard-animated answers to the money questions everyone wonders about.
Promise: "In under a minute, you'll finally understand why money works the way it does."
Audience: curious adults 18–45, mainly US/UK/CA/AU, not finance experts.

## Four pillars
1. Everyday prices: .99 prices, printer ink, AriZona tea at 99¢.
2. Countries and money: Monaco, the Swiss franc, printing money.
3. Business tricks: casinos, gyms, subscriptions, loyalty cards.
4. Money history: tulip mania, Zimbabwe's 100-trillion-dollar note.

## Format
- Shorts, 1 a day, 35–50 s.
  - 0–2 s: the question plus a shocking fact ("This tea has cost 99¢ since 1992.").
  - Middle: 2–3 reasons, each drawn on the whiteboard.
  - End: a twist, then "Follow for tomorrow's why."
- Long-form: 1 a week from week 2, 8–12 min, deep-diving the best Shorts' topics: hook, the question, 3–5 chapters,
  a surprising ending, and a tease for the next video.

## Style
- Same whiteboard pipeline as Borderline Why, with its own colours (black ink, money-green accents, red for "cost"),
  so the two channels look different.
- Signature: a hand-drawn price tag shows the question at the start of every video.
- A different TTS voice from Borderline Why: warm and curious, licensed for commercial use.

## Rules
- Explain, never advise (no "buy this" or "invest in that").
- An original script for every video; facts checked in 2+ sources, listed in the description.
- Images: the owner's own drawings or free-licensed images, credited where the licence requires.
- Vary topics so the channel never looks mass-produced.

## First videos
Shorts: AriZona 99¢; why a country can't print money to get rich; why casinos have no clocks or windows; why prices
end in .99; why Monaco is so rich with no resources.
First long-form: "Why Can't Countries Just Print Money?" (Weimar Germany, Zimbabwe, Venezuela).

## Channel description (draft)
"Why does that cost so much? Why can't countries just print money? Why do casinos never have clocks? Pricetag Why
answers the money questions you've always wondered about, with simple whiteboard animation and real stories. New
'why' every day. Not financial advice, just the fascinating reasons behind the prices."

## Check after 2 weeks
Shorts averaging 500+ views (Borderline Why's level), 3+ subscribers per 1,000 views, and the first long-form keeping
40%+ of viewers through the first 30 seconds.
